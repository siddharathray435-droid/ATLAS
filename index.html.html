Rules in this build
  • 15 s per turn, 0.5 s shorter every 3 rounds (floor 6 s).
  • Lose a life on timeout, Pass, or 3 wrong tries.
  • Pass → opponent gets a 6 s steal window (2 tries, +1 life).
  • Call Discussion once per match: Bot votes. A dead letter is cancelled free; a live one makes Bot answer or lose a life.
  • Cancelled letters are skipped: if a place ends in a cancelled letter, the next letter is the one before it (Namibia with A cancelled → I).
  • Max lives = start + 1. Leaving the tab twice on your turn forfeits it.
